About the King Fahd National Library

The King Fahd National Library is the only national library of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and among its objectives and tasks is the collection, organization, control, documentation, promotion, and dissemination of Saudi national intellectual output.

Objectives of the King Fahd National Library

  1. Collecting, organizing, and making available national intellectual output.
  2. Providing high-quality information services to all user groups using the latest technologies.
  3. Leading and developing the Saudi library and information science.
  4. Transitioning to e-government services.
  5. Enhancing the library's cultural and social role.
  6. Improving the library's performance efficiency.
