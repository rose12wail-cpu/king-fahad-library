Explore Our Services

📚 Loan Service
The King Fahd National Library provides a loan service that allows users to borrow books according to specific regulations, enabling readers and researchers to benefit from library resources outside the library for a limited period.
🖥️ Digital Services
The library provides advanced digital services, including access to electronic resources, online catalogs, and digital databases that support scientific research and knowledge access.
🔬 Research Support
Special services are provided for researchers, such as assistance in locating references, access to theses and manuscripts, and specialized information support.
📖 Reading Halls
The library offers well-equipped reading halls designed for reading and study. These halls provide a quiet and comfortable environment suitable for students and researchers.
📜 Legal Deposit
This service requires publishers and authors to deposit copies of their intellectual production at the King Fahd National Library, aiming to preserve and document the national intellectual output.
🎭 Cultural Events
Exhibitions and cultural programs supporting the scientific movement.
